# MaterialGraph Release 1 — Participant Briefing

**Revision:** rev1, 2026-10-17. Participant-facing.

## Read aloud before tasks (approximately one minute)

“Thank you for helping evaluate MaterialGraph. Please investigate alternatives starting from LiFePO4 as you would in an early research exploration. We want to learn what you understand from the results, what helps your work, and what evidence you would seek next. We are evaluating the tool, not your knowledge.

“This research prototype draws results from a selected, battery-relevant cohort; its results are not a comprehensive search of Materials Project, battery materials, or materials space. It displays source material records alongside MaterialGraph's rule-based analysis. Treat the suggestions as starting points for research, and check the cited source records and independent evidence before scientific decisions. You can tell us whenever something seems unclear or unsupported.”

## Standard think-aloud instruction

“Work through the tasks as you normally would. Please say what you are looking at, what you think the system is telling you, what you expect to happen, and what seems useful, unclear, surprising, or untrustworthy. There are no right answers we are testing you on.”

## Pilot access and normal server logs

Your initial session is observed by a facilitator. You operate MaterialGraph yourself; the facilitator observes your interpretation and provides only the assistance permitted by the protocol.

You receive your own pilot username and password. Your access can be withdrawn individually, without affecting other participants.

During the pilot, MaterialGraph records normal server access logs, which include your pilot username but never your password. We use these records for operational troubleshooting and to understand whether the prototype is used again during the evaluation period. A logged request alone does not establish meaningful or voluntary return use.

You retain access for approximately two weeks after the initial session. Returning independently is optional; there is no requirement to use MaterialGraph again. Keep your pilot credentials private.

Some investigations take several seconds to return results. If the service becomes unavailable, contact the facilitator. We will check the interruption and arrange continuation or rescheduling rather than ask you to retry repeatedly.
