# AMD-002: AI adjudication, founder spot-check, later domain verification

Status: DRAFT, takes effect when committed and tagged `rrb-01-amd-002`
Amends: AMD-001, section 3 (comparison and adjudication) and section 1 (study description)
Reason: the founder cannot judge the source texts reliably. Guessed human decisions
would make the answer key look checked when it is not. No adjudication decision had been

Everything not named here stays as locked (protocol, AMD-001, runs, comparison).

## 1. Study description (replaces AMD-001 section 1)

RRB-01 is an AI-extracted, AI-adjudicated, externally sourced retrospective substitution
retrieval evaluation. Two isolated AI extraction runs applied frozen rules to two
published reviews; a third AI adjudicated their disagreements and audited their agreed
records against the source PDFs using fixed textual checks. The founder spot-checked the
adjudication's textual claims. No materials-science expert has verified any decision.
All benchmark results are provisional until a domain adjudication has been completed.

## 2. Adjudicator

- A named AI model, run through Claude Code with the fixed brief
  `CLAUDE_CODE_BRIEF_adjudication.md`, committed before it is used.
- It receives only: the locked rules (`ai_extraction_rules.md`), the two frozen source PDFs,
  and the generated `comparison/adjudication.xlsx`. Nothing about expected or desired
  outcomes and nothing from the design discussions.
- Record: model identifier as reported by Claude Code, date, the brief as sent, and Claude
  Code's full report for each batch.
- Output: `comparison/adjudication_ai.xlsx`, a copy of `adjudication.xlsx` in which only the
  yellow columns are filled. In this file the `founder_*` columns hold the AI's decisions;
  `assistance_used` reads "performed by AI: <model identifier>". The founder does not edit
  the AI's decisions.

## 3. Fixed textual checks

For each row, the adjudicator answers four questions from the PDF page cited by the record:

1. Is the evidence quote on the cited page? Differences in whitespace, line breaks,
   hyphenation at line ends, ligatures and subscript rendering do not count as differences.
2. Do the recorded compositions appear in that passage as published?
3. Does the passage explicitly link the two compositions (for example "replaced by",
   "substituted", "instead of", "analog of", "doped with") and state which is the original?
4. Is either composition a variable, placeholder, family, or spatially heterogeneous material?

Decisions:

- **Unmatched records and normalization failures.** `include` only if a parent is named and
  checks 1–3 are yes and check 4 is no. `exclude`, with the locked exclusion reason, only
  when the text clearly shows that condition (no named parent, no stated direction,
  variable, family, morphology or surface only, spatially heterogeneous, synthesis route
  only). Otherwise `ambiguous`. If the quote is not found on the cited page: `ambiguous`,
  noting any other page where it was found.
- **Joint includes (audit).** `confirmed` if checks 1–3 are yes and check 4 is no for the
  recorded pair. Otherwise `error`, with `error_type` one of `evidence_not_found`,
  `composition_mismatch`, `no_explicit_direction`, `variable_or_family`, `other`, and the
  corrected decision.
- Every row records `rule_applied` and a `reason` stating the page checked and the answer to
  each of the four checks.

## 4. Founder spot-check

After the AI finishes, the founder checks rows ADJ-001, ADJ-010, ADJ-019, ADJ-028,
ADJ-037, ADJ-046, ADJ-055, ADJ-064, ADJ-073, ADJ-082 and ADJ-091 (every 9th row from the
first; selected before any AI decision exists). For each: open the PDF at the cited page,
search for the evidence quote, and record whether it is there and whether the AI's
answer to check 1 matches. Results go in `comparison/spotcheck_founder.md`. Spot-check
findings are reported; they do not silently change AI decisions. A failed spot-check is
reported as a limitation of the AI adjudication.

## 5. Later domain adjudication

When a materials-science domain person is available, they adjudicate the same rows
manually, recording their decisions in a separate workbook.
- If this is completed **before** any confirmatory benchmark request is run, the domain
  decisions replace the AI decisions in the cohort, recorded as such.
- If completed **after**, the domain review is reported alongside the original results as a
  post-hoc check. The cohort used for the pre-registered results is not changed.

4. Cohort construction (Stage 6) from the adjudicated list, as in the locked protocol.
